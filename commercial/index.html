Food-Service Hot Water Systems Built for the Rush

Dish machines, sanitizing sinks, and handwashing stations all compete for the same hot water, and a heater that's undersized or failing reveals itself as a dish machine that can't hit temperature and a backed-up dish room. A high-temperature dish machine usually needs final rinse water near 180°F, which usually means a booster heater working alongside the main system. We handle high-input tanks, tankless racks, booster heaters, and sizing for peak demand, with service scheduled around your hours.

Hot water temperature is a core concern for any food-service operator. Health inspectors check water temperature at handwashing sinks and sanitizing stations, and a dish machine that can't reach its rinse temperature puts the whole operation at risk. Our work keeps your system delivering the temperatures and volume your operation depends on, with written service records that demonstrate a well-maintained facility to health inspectors and landlords. We provide detailed reports after each visit, along with the records many leases and inspectors ask for.

We work directly with owners, managers, and kitchen staff to plan replacements and maintenance that protect your business and fit your operating schedule. Our technicians know how to work in occupied facilities, installing after close when needed, minimizing disruption, and keeping part of the system online where possible.

Code & Inspections

Safe Installations, Documented Service

Every commercial job we take on starts with safety. We inspect flue draft and vent connections, confirm proper combustion air, test relief valves and their discharge lines, confirm expansion tanks are charged correctly, and set mixing valves so water can be stored hot enough to control bacteria while reaching the tap at a safe temperature. Permits and inspections are handled as part of the job. You receive a written report with priority levels and specific recommended actions.

Good records matter almost as much as good repairs. Every account gets a documentation binder with detailed service logs, temperature readings, and relief valve test dates, so you can demonstrate due diligence to landlords, insurers, and inspectors. Equipment that works hardest, in kitchens, laundries, and large mechanical rooms, is put on a more frequent visit schedule.

Emergency Service

Rapid-Response Water Heater Repair for Businesses

Every hour without hot water costs your business money. In a restaurant, it can bring the dish room to a halt. In a salon, it cancels appointments. In a hotel, it brings refund requests, and in a multifamily building it leads to tenant disputes. Jackson Water Heater offers fast emergency water heater repair for commercial properties across the area, with after-hours scheduling for planned work so you don't lose a business day.

Maintenance Programs

Commercial Water Heater Maintenance Programs

A maintenance program from Jackson Water Heater gets built around your property's specific equipment, how the building uses hot water, and your budget. Visits include tank flushing and anode checks, descaling of tankless heat exchangers, relief valve testing, burner, flue, and gas valve inspection, recirculation pump and mixing valve checks, and temperature verification at the tap. Mineral-heavy water shortens the life of commercial equipment fast, so busy properties usually benefit from more frequent visits.

Next to an emergency water heater replacement, scheduled maintenance typically costs a fraction as much, and it sharply cuts the risk of downtime and tenant complaints. Businesses that commit to a program usually see fewer outages, longer equipment life, and lower energy bills. Give us a call to talk through a plan for your property and budget.
